# 2026-07-02 00:16 - Add answer page "How is MODBUS Handled Over UDP"

The user asked how MODBUS is handled over UDP and whether UDP is part of the MODBUS
standard. A search of the raw specifications in `raw/MODBUS/` found no occurrence of
"UDP", "datagram", or "connectionless". The official IP transport specification
(MODBUS Messaging on TCP/IP Implementation Guide) describes TCP only and is built around
TCP-specific mechanisms (connection establishment, connection pools, keep-alive,
retransmission).

The new answer page states clearly that UDP is not part of any official MODBUS
specification, marks non-spec content as "(convention, not in specs)", and grounds all
official statements in the spec-derived pages. It explains the practical (vendor)
convention of placing the MBAP header + PDU into a single UDP datagram, notes the port
502 TCP/UDP IANA distinction, and details why UDP's lack of reliability, ordering,
duplicate protection, and retransmission matters for a protocol that deliberately omits
an application-layer error check because it assumes TCP guarantees.

## Rationale

- Answers of general value are filed back into the wiki so they compound over time.
- The disclaimer and "not in specs" markers preserve the boundary between official
  specification content and common industry practice.
